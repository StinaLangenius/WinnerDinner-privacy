# WinnerDinner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

WinnerDinner is an app for deciding what to cook together. This page explains what the app stores and why.

## What we store

- The name you enter and the name of your household
- A household photo, if you add one
- Photos you add of meals you have cooked
- Your meal choices: swipes, matches and cooked history
- Your household preferences: things you avoid, maximum cooking time and tonight's mood

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no ads or tracking.

## How it is used

Your data is used only to make the app work: showing the same meals to both household members, finding matches, and keeping your cooked history.

## Who processes it

- **Supabase** hosts the database and photo storage.
- **Google Gemini** generates meal suggestions. It receives your household preferences (things you avoid, maximum cooking time, mood) and the names of meals already in your deck. It does not receive your name or your photos.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

## Deleting your data

In the app, go to Household, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete household. This permanently deletes the household and everything in it, including all photos, for both members.
